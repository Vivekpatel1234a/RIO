## Real-data 3D flood visualization (resources/ four files)

- Four files in `C:/Users/vivek/Downloads/Project_RIO/resources/` are real
  HEC-RAS 2D outputs: Depth@00:26:00, Depth (Max), WSE (Min) GeoTIFFs + one
  VRT whose source TIFF is MISSING (reported as unavailable, never faked).
- All rasters: EPSG:2271 (NAD83 / Pennsylvania North ftUS), 10 ft cells,
  NoData -9999, US survey feet. Domain ~35.9x25.7 km, lon/lat
  -77.781..-77.306, 40.949..41.196 (north-central PA, near Lock Haven).
- Deepest cell: 56.16 ft (17.12 m) at lon -77.61065, lat 41.04537.
- Backend: `app/services/flood/raster_pipeline.py` + `app/api/flood.py`.
  Endpoints: GET /api/flood/meta, /api/flood/state/{name}/image.png,
  /api/flood/state/{name}/grid. Converts ft->m, reprojects 2271->4326,
  downsamples to ~1000px, color-maps to RGBA PNGs, caches in
  backend/storage/flood_cache/. rasterio 1.5.1 + pillow added to venv.
- Frontend: `/flood-3d` page + `components/flood3d/FloodTerrainViewer.tsx`.
  MapLibre GL (already in project) 3D terrain from REAL DEM (Terrarium tiles,
  USGS 3DEP — external dependency, needs internet), flood layers as draped
  image sources, timeline baseline -> t26 -> max with labeled crossfade,
  play/pause/speed/reset camera/exaggeration slider/legend/provenance panel.
  Navbar got a "3D Flood" entry.
- Anti-vibe rules kept: no procedural terrain anywhere; flood pixels are exact
  raster values (unit-converted/reprojected only). Vertical exaggeration and
  snapshot crossfade are labeled as visual aids in the UI.
